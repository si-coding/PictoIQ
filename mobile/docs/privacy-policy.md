# PictoIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

PictoIQ ("the app") helps you find the best shot among similar photos and remove exact duplicates, on your own phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Your photos stay on your device

- PictoIQ reads photos only from the folder you choose with Android's folder picker. It cannot see any other folder.
- Photos are analysed on your device. They are **not uploaded** to us or to anyone else.
- There is **no account** and no sign-in.
- PictoIQ does not show ads.

## What the app does with your photos

- It creates small previews and numerical fingerprints (such as colour and sharpness measurements) to group similar photos and suggest the best one. These are kept in memory while the app is open and are not saved or sent anywhere.
- It detects whether faces are present, whether eyes are open and whether people are smiling, using Google ML Kit's on-device face detection. It does **not** identify who anyone is and does not compare people with each other beyond counting faces and comparing clothing colours inside the same scan.
- It deletes a photo **only** after you confirm in the app. Deleted photos are removed from the folder you chose.

## Information stored on your device

The app saves your settings (for example the match level you picked) on your device. Nothing else is saved between scans.

## Information sent to Google by ML Kit

The face detection component (Google ML Kit) sends limited technical diagnostics to Google over an encrypted connection, as described in [Google's ML Kit data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure). This can include the app's package name and version, device model and Android version, performance measurements and a per-installation identifier that is not intended to identify you or your device. It does not include your photos or the faces in them. We do not receive this information.

## Sharing

We do not sell your information and do not share it with third parties. We do not collect it in the first place: the only data leaving your device is the ML Kit diagnostics above, which go to Google.

## Children

PictoIQ is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children.
