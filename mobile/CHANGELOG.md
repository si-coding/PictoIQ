# PictoIQ changelog

One line per build uploaded to Google Play. The number after the + is the Android version code and must go up with every upload.

| Build | Track | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0.0+4 | Internal | Best moments now need a clearly visible, in-focus person and no quality problems, even for a photo that is the only one of its moment; scenery and weak single photos are no longer shown, whatever the content filter says. People must fill at least 0.6% of the picture (0.4% for two or more people), so full-length couples and groups still qualify. Glasses and wide smiles no longer count as closed eyes; the reasons now show the real face size and eye reading. Best moments settings no longer offer a Scenery choice (Best moments are about people); changing the filter for Best shots no longer affects Best moments. Best moments settings show an "Always about people" panel instead of a chip. Leaving a results screen with Back, the search icon, Change filter or the system Back now asks "Leave these results?" first. Settings screen has the same top bar as the other screens. "License" spelled with an s. |
| 1.0.0+3 | Internal | Face score weighted by group size; the scan clock ticks every second with units (no time-left guess); friendly no-results screen whose buttons open the calendar or album picker and return to the tool; (i) reasons for photos not grouped; light tinted sunglasses recognised; Best shots "Same people only" setting (on by default, now also stops chains of look-alikes joining different people); photos of people with their backs turned are no longer offered on their own in Best moments; no "eyes look open" praise for tiny faces. |
| 1.0.0+2 | Internal | Sunglasses are no longer treated as closed eyes in Best shots and Best moments. |
| 1.0.0+1 | Internal (23 Nov 2026) | First internal test build. |
