# PictoIQ changelog

One line per build uploaded to Google Play. The number after the + is the Android version code and must go up with every upload.

| Build | Track | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.0.0+3 | Internal | Face score weighted by group size; the scan clock ticks every second with units (no time-left guess); friendly no-results screen whose buttons open the calendar or album picker and return to the tool; (i) reasons for photos not grouped; light tinted sunglasses recognised; Best shots "Same people only" setting (on by default, now also stops chains of look-alikes joining different people); photos of people with their backs turned are no longer offered on their own in Best moments; no "eyes look open" praise for tiny faces. |
| 1.0.0+2 | Internal | Sunglasses are no longer treated as closed eyes in Best shots and Best moments. |
| 1.0.0+1 | Internal (23 Nov 2026) | First internal test build. |
